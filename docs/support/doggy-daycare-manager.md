Frequently Asked Questions

How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings → Data Management → Export Backup. You can save to Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I restore from a backup?
Go to Settings → Data Management → Import Backup, then select your backup file.

How does iCloud sync work?
When enabled, your data automatically syncs across your devices using your private iCloud account. Go to Settings → iCloud Sync to check status.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?
Yes. Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device and enable CloudKit sync. Your data will stay in sync automatically.

How do I archive a dog instead of deleting?
Open the dog's profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Archive." Archived dogs are hidden from daily views but preserved in your data.

How do I permanently delete a dog?
Go to Settings → Data Management → Manage Archived Dogs, select the dog, and choose Delete.

Why isn't my data syncing?
Check that iCloud is enabled in your device Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Doggy Daycare Manager. Also ensure you have an internet connection.

Is my data secure?
Yes. Data is stored locally on your device and optionally in your private iCloud account. We never have access to your data and never share it with third parties.

What happens if I delete the app?
Local data is removed. If you had CloudKit sync enabled, your data remains in iCloud and will restore when you reinstall.
